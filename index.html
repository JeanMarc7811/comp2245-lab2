Why I Enjoy Learning Technology

October 4, 2026 | By Jean-Marc de Gannes

Technology has become an important part of everyday life. Learning how software and websites are created gives me a better understanding of the technology that I use every day. Programming also provides opportunities to solve problems and create useful applications.

One of the things I enjoy most about learning technology is seeing the results of something I have built. With continued practice, I can develop better programming skills and eventually create larger and more useful projects.
